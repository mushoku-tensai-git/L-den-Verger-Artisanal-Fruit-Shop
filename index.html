The 4-Pillar Farm-to-Palate Vow

Why our fruit graces the menus of Three-Star Michelin tables and private luxury banquets worldwide.

Pillar I

Single-Estate Heritage

No mass-aggregation. Every crate links to a single registered grower in microclimates like Yamanashi, Ratnagiri, and Syracuse.

Pillar II

Active Thermal Vault

Proprietary insulated cellular packs keep pulp at an unbroken 4°C, halting cellular degradation without chemical ripening gases.

Pillar III

Brix & Acidity Index

Optical laser refractor metrics determine picking hour. Only fruits scoring within the top 3% peak brix percentile are packed.

Pillar IV

Compostable Luxury

Zero single-use synthetics. Vegetable-based cushioning, reusable keepsake Paulownia boxes, and FSC-certified soy-printed wraps.
